# Version 0.4.2
- **Breaking**: the API is rebuilt around the parts of the map-maker, without aliases of the former names (`docs/source/migration.rst` maps every former name to the new one):
  `HMaps` holds the orientation functions and the hits of a detector; it reads and writes the h-map files of litebird_sim and Falcons.jl (`HMaps.load`, `HMaps.save`, which writes the coordinate system with the names of litebird_sim and always writes `duration_s` and `sampling_rate_Hz`), reads the cross-link files of SBM 0.4 (`HMaps.load_legacy`), is computed from samples (`HMaps.from_samples`), and combines detectors with the same polarization angle (`HMaps.combine`).
  `SignalModel` holds the spin components of the data; models add up, and the product of two models is the model of the product of their signals. `Basis` gives the components solved for, formerly `mdim` (`Basis.qu`, `Basis.iqu`, `.extend([...])` for the templates).
  `Sky` holds I and P with their spin derivatives and the second-order maps (`Sky.from_maps`, `Sky.from_alm`, or derivatives given to `Sky`).
  `map_make((hmaps, model), basis)` solves every pixel at once; with one `(HMaps, SignalModel)` per detector it sums their systems weighted by the hits. `MapMaker` reuses the inverse for many models, `orientation_matrix` replaces `create_covmat`, `noise_covariance` and `noise_maps` give the white noise.
  The systematics are functions of `sbm.systematics` that do not know the scan and return the perturbation of the signal, to be added to `sky_signal`: `diff_gain`, `diff_pointing` and `abs_pointing` (with `second_order`), `hwp_wedge`, `hwp_ip`, `hwp_mueller` (a non-ideal HWP Mueller matrix with harmonics in the HWP angle, in litebird_sim's chain; `hwp_ip` is one of its cases), `bandpass_mismatch`, `polarization_angle` (an error on the polarization angle, with and without HWP) and `beam`. Angles are the arguments ending in `_rad`.
  `ScanFields`, `SignalFields`, `Field`, `Convolver`, `elliptical_beam`, the pipelines (`Configlation`, `Systematics`, `sim_*`, `generate_maps`), the detector database, `install_db`, `get_instrument_table`, `pa45` and `t2b` are removed; `tools` is split into `spectra`, `likelihood` and `plotting`.
- **Breaking**: only the `"telescope"` HWP angle is kept, that of the h-maps of litebird_sim and Falcons.jl: the argument `hwp_angle_frame` and the `"legacy"` and `"sky"` conventions are removed. `hwp_wedge` and `hwp_ip` are the wedge and the HWP-IP of the `"telescope"` convention, and the phase of `hwp_ip` is that of Patanchon et al. (2024), `epsilon I cos(4 phi + phi_QI)`, as with `hwp_ip_field(..., hwp_angle_frame="telescope")`.
- **Breaking**: the unsolved pixels of the map-maker are `NaN` instead of 0, and the pixels to solve are explicit: by default the observed pixels whose system is well conditioned (`solvable`), to which a `mask=` is restricted, with a warning for the pixels it drops. `HMaps.crosslink_mask(threshold)` reproduces the former selection without HWP.
- **Breaking**: the derivatives of the polarization are covariant (spin-weighted) derivatives, which `Sky` computes with spin transforms of the E and B coefficients (`sbm.sky.spin_derivatives`), and the second-order maps are the covariant second derivatives, computed exactly in the same way. A pointing offset displaces the beam rigidly, so its polarization direction is carried along; the derivatives of Q and U as scalar maps, which the former field builders were given (from healpy's `alm2map_der1` in the examples), omit the rotation of the local basis, a first-order error of order `cot(theta) P` that is largest near the poles. Checked against a Gaussian beam centred off the boresight: the residual of the pointing model falls as rho^2 at first order and rho^3 at second order, and as rho with the derivatives of Q and U as scalar maps. Derivatives given to `Sky` are used as they are, and `second_order_maps(..., transport=False)` gives second-order maps from finite differences in the local basis of the displaced points, consistent with derivatives of Q and U as scalar maps.
- **Breaking**: `forecast` has no `method`, `r0` and `tol`: the bias is always the exact maximum of the likelihood, and the MIGRAD minimisation is removed with the dependency iminuit. `forecast` raises `ValueError` for a spectrum with non-finite values (from `NaN` pixels) instead of returning a vanishing bias.
- the beam convolution is `sbm.beam.convolve`, which returns `{(n, m): map}` with and without HWP. `SphericalHarmonics(values, lmax, mmax)` has the attribute names of litebird_sim's class (whose objects, and arrays, are accepted), and the beams are built by `sbm.beam.pencil`, `sbm.beam.gaussian` (the pencil beam times the transfer functions of `healpy.gauss_beam`) and `sbm.beam.elliptical`. `hwp_lowell.py` is folded into `beam.py`, and `lowell_hwp="shifted"` / `"full_sum"` are `low_ell="narrow_beam"` / `"beam_sum"`. Without HWP, the moments above `lmax` are zero maps (`Convolver` called healpy with a spin above `lmax`, which aborts the process).
- fix the white-noise maps: the components were drawn independently, with twice the standard deviation of Q and U for an ideal scan; `noise_maps` draws them with the covariance of the map-maker and takes a `numpy.random.Generator`.
- to add a systematic: `sbm.systematics` is a package that follows the path of the signal through the instrument (`sbm/systematics/{sky,pointing,optics,hwp,detector}.py`), and each of its signal models (the sky signal and the systematics) is declared with `@systematic(data=..., templates=[...])`, so that `f.templates` gives the couples to add to the basis (`Basis.extend(sy.abs_pointing.templates)`) and `sbm.systematics.ALL` lists them all. `sbm.testing` provides the tools to check a model against TOD (`random_samples`, `tod_from_components`, `least_squares`, `assert_matches_tod`, `RealSky`); `CONTRIBUTING.md`, the documentation page "Adding a systematic" and `notebooks/adding_a_systematic.ipynb` explain the steps.
- the tests compare every model with an independent computation (TOD in real space fitted by least squares, the sky sampled at the offset pointing, litebird_sim's TOD model), check that the templates of every systematic absorb its first-order perturbation, fail if a systematic has no test case or is missing from the documentation, and use no reference maps.
- SBM no longer depends on litebird_sim: the fiducial spectra are shipped in `sbm/datautils`. The dependencies are NumPy (1.x or 2.x), healpy, h5py and ducc0 (0.40 or later; the Wigner 3j symbols of `low_ell="beam_sum"` come from `ducc0.misc.wigner3j_int` instead of sympy), and matplotlib is optional (`get_cmap`, extra `plot`). The packaging uses hatchling; the CI runs on Linux with NumPy 1 and 2 and builds the documentation without deploying it.
- the documentation and the tutorials (`notebooks/systematics_tutorial.ipynb`, `notebooks/beam_tutorial.ipynb`, `notebooks/adding_a_systematic.ipynb`) are written for the new API.
- fix `Convolver` without HWP for spin moments above `lmax`: it called healpy's `alm2map_spin` for every moment `k` of `spin_k`, also for `k > lmax`, and libsharp aborted the process ("bad spin"), e.g. with `lmax=3` and `spin_k=[0, 2, 4]`. A spin-k map has no multipole below k, so these moments are now zero maps, as the path with HWP already returned. The test runs the convolution in a subprocess, with and without HWP.
- fix the derivatives of the polarization for the pointing systematics: `sim_diff_pointing_per_ch` differentiated Q and U as scalar maps with `healpy.alm2map_der1`, which omits the rotation of the local basis (a term of order `cot(theta) P`, largest near the poles), so that the first-order term of the pointing offsets was wrong. A pointing offset displaces the beam rigidly and carries its polarization direction along, so the derivatives of `P` are the covariant (spin-weighted) ones. The new function `spin_derivatives(alm, nside)` returns `eth I`, `eth P` and `eth_bar P` from the T, E, B coefficients with spin transforms (a spin above `lmax` gives a zero map), the pipeline uses it, and these are the maps to give to `SignalFields.diff_pointing_field`, `abs_pointing_field` and `circular_pointing_field`. Checked against an ell = 2 E-mode (`eth P = 0` and `eth_bar P = -4i c sin(theta) cos(theta)` with `c = sqrt(15/(2 pi))/4`, whereas the scalar derivatives give `+-2i c sin(theta) cos(theta)`) and against central differences of the sky along geodesics with the polarization transported along the displacement; `eth I` equals that of `alm2map_der1`.
- fix the standard deviations of the noise maps of `ScanFields.generate_noise`: Q and U were drawn with `sigma_P = sqrt(2) sigma_I` times the square root of the diagonal element `(A^-1)_PP` of the inverse map-making matrix, which is `Var(Q) + Var(U)` in units of `sigma_I^2`, so that their standard deviation was twice the correct one for an ideal scan (2.83 `sigma_I` instead of `sqrt(2) sigma_I`). Each of I, Q and U now has the standard deviation of the covariance `sigma_I^2 A^-1` of the map-maker, `Var(Q), Var(U) = sigma_I^2 ((A^-1)_PP +- Re (A^-1)_PP*) / 2`, checked against the least-squares fit of TOD with white noise, with and without HWP and templates; the components are still drawn independently, without their correlations. I does not change, and Q and U are drawn from the same random numbers (they are about halved). The reference maps `tests/reference/noise_map_100ukrts_{hwp_mdim_{2,3,4},mdim_{3,5}}.fits` are regenerated. The docstring of `generate_noise_pdf` explains `scale`: the pair difference `(d_T - d_B)/2` of two detectors with the NET `net_ukrts` has the variance `NET^2 f_s / 2` per sample, hence `scale=2.0` with the hit map of one detector of the pair.
- fix `forecast` for spectra with non-finite values: a `NaN` in `cl_syst` or `n_el` (e.g. from `NaN` pixels of the maps) made every comparison of the bisection false, so that the bias came out as about 1e-119 instead of an error (and `method="minuit"` returned a meaningless value). `forecast` now raises `ValueError` if `cl_syst` or `n_el` has a non-finite value between ell = 2 and `lmax`.
- fix `ScanFields.map_make` and `ScanFields.generate_noise` in pixels with an ill-conditioned system: the pixels to solve (the observed pixels with HWP, the pixels whose cross-link `|h_2|` is below `xlink_threshold` without HWP) were solved even when their system was singular or ill-conditioned, e.g. a pixel seen with only two distinct crossing angles, which gave meaningless values (or `LinAlgError`) in the maps and huge values in the noise. A pixel whose system has a reciprocal condition number (2-norm) at or below `RCOND_MIN = 1e-12` is now not solved, with a warning, and is 0 in the maps and in the noise, the value of the unobserved pixels. The other pixels do not change (bit for bit on the test scan).
- fix the sign of the HWP-IP phase `phi_qi` in the `"telescope"` HWP-angle convention of `SignalFields.hwp_ip_field`: its route `epsilon/2 exp(-i phi_qi) T` sat at spin (0,4) and gave `epsilon cos(4 chi - phi_qi)`, with `chi` the focal-plane HWP angle counted in the standard sense (litebird_sim's HWP angle), whereas `"sky"`, `"legacy"` and Patanchon et al. (2024) give `epsilon cos(4 chi + phi_qi)`. The route now sits at (0,-4) (`hwp_ip_route_spins("telescope") == ((0, -4), (0, 4))`), so that `phi_qi` has the same meaning in every convention. Results with `phi_qi = 0` do not change (bit for bit with `mdim=3`). Checked against a TOD simulated with the physical angles in every convention.
- fix `forecast`: the bias on r is the maximum of the likelihood in r, but MIGRAD with `tol=1e-8` resolves it only to about 1e-9 and returns `rmin=1e-10` when the maximum is below it (a bias of 4.2e-9 came out as 1.35e-9). The new default `method="exact"` finds the root of the derivative of the negative log-likelihood by bisection over 0 <= r <= rmax to a relative precision of 1e-12, and returns 0 when the likelihood decreases from r = 0. `method="minuit"` keeps the previous behaviour; `rmin` is now only its lower limit and the scale of the displayed likelihood grid when the bias is zero (a non-positive `rmin` no longer collapses that grid to 0). The tests recover an injected tensor bias of 1e-2, 1e-5 and 1e-9 to 1e-9 relative, with and without noise.
- fix the HWP angle convention of `SignalFields.circular_pointing_field` (HWP wedge) and `SignalFields.hwp_ip_field`: the HWP-IP at spin (4,-4) assumes that the HWP angle `phi` of the scan fields is measured on the sky, while the wedge offset along `psi + phi + chi` assumes a focal-plane angle counted like `psi`, so the identity IP = 2 pol - 4 wedge_T between spin vectors failed. The argument `hwp_angle_frame` selects a consistent convention: `"sky"` (HWP angle on the sky as in Patanchon et al. 2024; wedge along `-phi + chi`, HWP-IP at (4,-4)) or `"telescope"` (HWP mounted on the telescope, the convention of the h-maps read by `ScanFields.load_lbs_h_maps`; wedge along `psi - phi + chi`, HWP-IP at (0,+-4)), with the offset direction and `chi` counted like SBM's crossing angle without HWP. Both are checked against a TOD simulated with the physical angles. The default `"legacy"` keeps the previous behaviour bit for bit. The helpers `wedge_offset_direction_spin` and `hwp_ip_route_spins` give the spins of each convention.
- fix the weights of `ScanFields.create_covmat`: they depended on `spin_n` alone (1 for `n == 0`), whereas `SignalFields.build_linear_system` halves every coupled field except (0,0). Basis components with `n == 0` and `m != 0`, such as a template at (0,+-4) for a signal that depends on the HWP angle alone, were weighted inconsistently and the solution leaked. The weight is now 1 only for (0,0); the existing bases do not change.
- fix `SignalFields.diff_pointing_field` (pair difference without HWP): the polarization-gradient terms were twice too large. In `d = (d_T - d_B)/2` the polarization of each detector enters with weight 1/2, so the weight of the `o_zeta` terms of the spin-1 and spin-3 fields is `-1/8`, not `-1/4`; the temperature-gradient term `-1/4 zeta eth I` was already correct. Checked against a direct TOD simulation of the offset pointings (the residual of `mdim=4` agrees with slope 0.997, 0.498 before). The reference maps `tests/reference/diff_pointing_output_map_mdim_{2,4,6}.fits` are regenerated.
- add `ScanFields.load_lbs_h_maps(file_paths)`, which reads the h-maps written by litebird_sim (`make_h_maps` / `save_h_maps`) and by Falcons.jl (`save_h_maps`) in litebird_sim's layout `h_maps_det_<name>.h5`, one file or several files combined by hit-weighted averaging. litebird_sim defines `h_{n,m} = <exp(+i(n psi + m chi))>`; SBM's signal model equals litebird_sim's TOD model, rotating HWP and general Mueller matrix included, when SBM's crossing angle is `-psi`, so the reader stores `h~_{n,m} = h^lbs_{n,-m}`. The h-maps do not include the detector polarization angle, so combine only detectors with the same polarization angle. String attributes are decoded whether they are variable-length (litebird_sim) or fixed-length (Falcons.jl, read by h5py as `bytes`).
- expose general asymmetric beam convolution as `SignalFields.beam_field()`; retain `elliptical_beam_field()` as a compatibility alias.
- add exception in the DB_ROOT_PATH setting in `scan_fields.py` to avoid to be down after import sbm.
- fix the map-based beam convolution: normalization of `elliptical_beam()`, the missing factor 1/2 of the polarized beam term in the spin-0 field of `SignalFields.elliptical_beam_field()`, and odd/negative spin `k` in `Convolver`. `elliptical_beam_field()` takes `spin_k` to include higher spin moments of the beam.
